Leo Mejillano Salon

Multi-branch booking platform · Capstone · 2026

With Angelo Navarra & Paulo Balaguer · March – December 2026

A web portal for Leo Mejillano Salon & Beauty where customers browse services across three branches in Albay, book and pay online, and track their appointment. Staff manage bookings through a separate Team Portal.

Key features

  • Online appointment booking
  • Three salon branches in Albay
  • Service & stylist selection
  • Appointment scheduling
  • Multiple payment options
  • Email or SMS verification
  • Booking tracking
  • Account registration & login
  • Home & event service requests

Built with

  • PHP 8
  • MySQL / MariaDB
  • HTML
  • Tailwind CSS
  • JavaScript
  • PayMongo
  • Brevo email
  • textbee SMS

My contribution & skills

  • Web development with PHP 8 and MySQL
  • Responsive front end with HTML, Tailwind CSS and JavaScript
  • Online payments through PayMongo, plus email and SMS one-time codes
  • Role-based access for five user panels
  • System planning and analysis
  • UI/UX design: booking flows, portals and layouts
  • Wireframing, data flow diagrams and flowcharts
  • Automated tests for bookings, payments and checkout, with a mock PayMongo server
  • Deploying with Docker to Vercel, with scheduled email, SMS and reminder jobs
  • Technical documentation, including the system's user manual
  • Team collaboration and project presentation
