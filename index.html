Capstone project · 2026

Centralized Digital Multi-Branch Platform for Leo Mejillano Salon and Beauty Services

March 2026 – December 2026 · STI College Legazpi · Project team: Michael Kharl Agao, Angelo and Paulo

A web-based multi-branch portal for Leo Mejillano Salon & Beauty that helps customers explore salon services, view branch information and manage appointment bookings. It includes guest and client booking, branch and staff selection, appointment scheduling, payment details, booking tracking and customer feedback, with separate Client Portal and Team Portal access for customer and staff functions. The project focuses on a responsive, organized and user-friendly experience for salon customers and branch operations.

Key features

  • Online appointment booking
  • Three salon branches in Albay
  • Service & stylist selection
  • Appointment scheduling
  • Multiple payment options
  • Email or SMS verification
  • Booking tracking
  • Account registration & login
  • Home & event service requests

E-Sumbong

Local Governance and Community Issue Resolution System

Oct 14 – Dec 1, 2025
STI College LegazpiDesktop Application

A desktop application that gives barangay residents and the Local Government Unit (LGU) one structured channel for community concerns. Residents log in to file reports, track their status and read official announcements, while barangay officials manage reports, update their status and post announcements.

Key modules

  • Role-based log in
  • Resident & Admin Portals
  • Report issue with landmark & priority
  • Manage Reports
  • Pending → In Progress → Resolved
  • My Reports
  • Live Issues Feed
  • Announcement Posting

Built with
